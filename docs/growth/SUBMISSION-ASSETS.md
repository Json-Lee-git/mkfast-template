## Long Description

AEOCheck helps SaaS, AI tool, B2B, marketing, and SEO teams inspect whether public pages expose the technical and editorial signals that AI-assisted discovery systems can use. The free tools check robots.txt, AI crawler access, sitemap discovery, LLMs.txt and LLMs-full.txt availability, structured data, headings, direct answers, entity clarity, and visible trust signals.

The product is intentionally conservative. It does not claim to predict rankings or guarantee AI citations. It helps teams find practical readiness gaps before they invest in broader SEO, AEO, GEO, or manual audit work.
