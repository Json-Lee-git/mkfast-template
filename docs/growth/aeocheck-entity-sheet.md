# AEOCheck Entity Sheet

Use this sheet as the source of truth for AEOCheck wording across website copy, directory submissions, external posts, outreach, and GEO monitoring notes.

## Canonical Identity

- Product name: AEOCheck
- Canonical domain: https://aeocheck.xyz
- Primary category: AI search readiness and answer engine optimization audit tool
- Short description: AEOCheck helps SaaS, AI tool, and B2B teams audit whether their websites are ready for AI search, answer engines, and AI crawlers.
- Primary CTA: Run the free AEO checker
- Paid offer: Manual AI Search Readiness Audit

## Target Users

- SaaS founders who rely on search-driven demand
- AI tool teams that need their product pages to be understandable by answer engines
- B2B marketers preparing pages for Google AI results, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and similar AI-assisted discovery surfaces
- SEO consultants who need a focused AI search readiness workflow for clients

## Core Differentiators

- Focuses on AI search readiness instead of generic SEO scoring.
- Checks technical access signals such as robots.txt, sitemap exposure, canonical URLs, and AI crawler visibility.
- Reviews answer-ready structure such as direct answers, FAQ blocks, comparison tables, schema, and clear entity details.
- Connects free checks with a paid manual audit workflow for teams that want deeper review.
- Publishes sample reports and methodology-oriented content so prospects can inspect the workflow before buying.

## Reusable One-Liners

- AEOCheck helps teams find the technical, content, and trust gaps that can make websites harder for AI search systems to understand.
- AEOCheck is built for AI search readiness checks, not broad all-purpose SEO reporting.
- The free checker gives teams a fast first pass; the manual audit provides a deeper human review and prioritized next steps.
- AEOCheck is useful when a team wants to inspect whether product pages are crawlable, answer-ready, and clear enough for AI-assisted discovery.

## Positioning Boundaries

- Do not promise guaranteed rankings, guaranteed AI citations, guaranteed traffic, guaranteed revenue, or guaranteed inclusion in AI answers.
- Do not claim direct partnerships or privileged access to Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, DeepSeek, ByteDance, or other AI/search providers.
- Do not position AEOCheck as a replacement for all SEO work. It is a focused readiness layer for AI search and answer engines.
- Do not claim that llms.txt alone creates AI visibility. Treat it as one possible clarity signal among many.
