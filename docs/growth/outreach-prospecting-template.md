Purpose: prepare the first manual outreach batch for free tool feedback, SEO/GEO traffic, brand exposure, and backlink opportunities.

Rule: this is preparation only. Do not send messages, submit forms, deploy, run production smoke tests, or validate paid order delivery from this workflow.

Build a first list of 60 prospects, then send or queue the first 20 messages when the operator is at the computer.

## Relevance Scoring

Score each prospect from 1 to 5.

5: Directly writes, sells, or curates technical SEO, AI search, AEO, GEO, LLMs.txt, AI crawler, or schema resources.

4: Works in SEO, SaaS marketing, content, or growth and has a clear reason to test AI search readiness.

3: Broadly relevant marketer, founder, or developer, but no explicit AI search or technical SEO angle.

2: Weak relevance; only use if they run a broad tool/resource list.

1: Skip.

Only contact score 4 or 5 prospects in the first batch.

## First 20 Prospect Mix

Use this mix for the first send batch:

- 8 SEO consultants or agencies
- 5 B2B SaaS founders or marketers
- 4 newsletter, roundup, or resource page owners

SEO consultants are most likely to provide high-signal critique. SaaS founders test clarity. Resource owners create backlink opportunities. Technical writers catch implementation framing issues.

## First Batch Workflow

1. Search one segment at a time.
2. Add prospects to the table until there are 60 rows.
3. Score relevance from 1 to 5.
4. Filter to score 4 or 5 only.
5. Select the first 20 using the segment mix above.
6. Personalize only the first sentence and one topic field.
7. Send or queue messages.
8. Follow up after 4-7 days if no response.
9. Record replies, tool runs, backlinks, and objections.

## Personalization Fields

Use lightweight personalization only:

- `{{name}}`
- `{{topic}}`: a recent post, service page, resource list, or visible area of expertise.
- `{{site}}`: their company, blog, or client niche.
- `{{reason}}`: why they are relevant to AI search readiness.

Avoid long custom intros. The goal is useful feedback, not over-optimized cold email.

## Quality Rules

- Do not contact irrelevant people just to hit volume.
- Do not imply AEOCheck guarantees AI citations or ranking gains.
- Do not pitch the paid manual audit in the first message.
- Do not send the same message to many people on the same platform too quickly.
- Stop a segment if replies show the positioning is unclear; revise copy before sending more.

## Objection Handling

If they say AI search readiness is too speculative:
Reply that AEOCheck separates readiness from visibility and only checks observable technical/editorial signals.

If they ask whether LLMs.txt is required:
Reply that it is not a ranking guarantee or universal requirement; AEOCheck treats it as one optional AI-readable discovery signal.

If they say this is just SEO:
Reply that many checks overlap with technical SEO, but the output is organized around crawlability, extractability, entity clarity, and answer readiness.

If they ask for proof of output:
Share the sample report: https://aeocheck.xyz/sample-aeo-report

If they ask about scoring assumptions:
Share the methodology: https://aeocheck.xyz/methodology
