# AEOCheck Marketing Execution Pack

Focus: free tool user growth, long-term SEO/GEO traffic, brand exposure, and backlink groundwork.

This pack is for work that can be prepared before publishing. It avoids deployment, smoke tests, paid-order validation, login-only submissions, and any action that requires the operator to be at the computer.

## Positioning

Primary offer: Free tools to check technical readiness for AI search, answer engines, and AI crawler discovery.

Primary CTA: https://aeocheck.xyz/tools/aeo-checker

Secondary links:
- Checklist: https://aeocheck.xyz/guides/ai-search-readiness-checklist
- Sample report: https://aeocheck.xyz/sample-aeo-report
- Methodology: https://aeocheck.xyz/methodology
- References: https://aeocheck.xyz/references
- GEO audit tool: https://aeocheck.xyz/tools/geo-audit

Claims guardrails:
- Say the tools check readiness signals.
- Do not promise rankings, traffic, AI citations, Google AI Overview inclusion, ChatGPT mentions, or revenue.
- Ask for feedback on false positives, missing crawler rules, scoring weights, and practical usefulness.

## 7-Day Execution Plan

### Day 1: Baseline And Directory Prep
Goal: prepare submissions that can be pasted into directories and communities.

Tasks:
- Pick 10 candidate directories or communities from the channel queue.
- Prepare one row per candidate in the tracking table.
- Choose one target URL per row: homepage, AEO checker, checklist, sample report, or methodology.

Success metric: 10 ready-to-submit rows with URL, category, description, and status.

### Day 2: SEO Tool And AI Tool Directories
Goal: get AEOCheck into relevant list pages and resource directories.

Tasks:
- Submit or queue 5 directory listings.
- Prefer SEO tools, AI marketing tools, webmaster tools, content marketing, and technical SEO categories.
- Use short descriptions first. Use long descriptions only where supported.

Success metric: 5 submitted or fully prepared directory rows.

### Day 3: Community Feedback Posts
Goal: create useful feedback-driven posts that can earn comments and early users.

Tasks:
- Publish or queue 2 community posts.
- Use Reddit, Hacker News, Indie Hackers, X, or LinkedIn drafts.
- Frame the post as a free diagnostic tool and request feedback.

Success metric: 2 posts submitted or ready for posting, each with a specific feedback question.

### Day 4: SEO/GEO Article Syndication
Goal: turn the checklist into external article exposure.

Tasks:
- Prepare one Medium, Hashnode, LinkedIn article, or newsletter pitch.
- Link back to the checklist and AEO checker.
- Keep the canonical source on aeocheck.xyz when the platform supports canonical URLs.

Success metric: 1 article queued or published, with canonical/backlink noted.

### Day 5: Founder And SEO Consultant Outreach
Goal: get targeted feedback from people who can use or share the tool.

Tasks:
- Build a list of 20 prospects: SEO consultants, B2B SaaS founders, content leads, or technical marketers.
- Send or queue 10 concise messages.
- Ask for a 2-minute audit run and one piece of feedback.

Success metric: 20 prospects identified, 10 messages sent or staged.

### Day 6: Backlink And Roundup Pitches
Goal: create external evidence around AEOCheck as a resource.

Tasks:
- Find 10 pages that list SEO tools, AI marketing tools, LLMs.txt resources, or GEO resources.
- Pitch AEOCheck only where it genuinely fits.
- Use the most relevant deep URL.

Success metric: 10 candidate pages logged, 5 pitches sent or queued.

### Day 7: Review And Next Batch
Goal: decide what to repeat.

Tasks:
- Review rows by status: prepared, submitted, live, rejected, needs follow-up.
- Record referral visits, tool starts, comments, replies, and backlinks if analytics are available.
- Pick the next highest-signal channel for another 7-day cycle.

Success metric: one clear next channel: directories, communities, outreach, article syndication, or backlink pitches.
