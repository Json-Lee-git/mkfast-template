---
title: FAQ Schema
description: Structured data that marks up a list of questions and answers on a page, enabling rich results in search and making Q&A content extractable by AI models.
---

**FAQ schema** uses the `FAQPage` Schema.org type containing a list of `Question` and `Answer` items. When Google displays FAQ rich results, the questions appear as expandable accordions directly in search results, increasing the page's visual footprint.

## Why it matters for AI search

FAQ schema makes question-answer pairs machine-readable. AI models can extract these pairs directly for use in AI Overviews, voice search answers, and chatbot responses. Pages with well-structured FAQ markup are more likely to be sourced for direct answers.

## Best practices

- Only mark up questions and answers that are visible on the page
- Each answer should be complete and self-contained
- Don't use FAQ schema for advertising or rhetorical questions
- Avoid duplicate question-answer pairs across pages
