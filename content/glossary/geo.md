---
title: GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
description: The practice of optimizing content specifically for AI-powered generative search and response engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.
---

**GEO** (Generative Engine Optimization) focuses on making content discoverable and citable by generative AI search products. Unlike traditional SEO, which targets search engine result pages, GEO targets the answers that AI systems generate from web content.

## GEO vs AEO vs SEO

- **SEO**: Optimize for Google search rankings
- **AEO**: Optimize for answer engine comprehension
- **GEO**: Optimize for generative AI citation and inclusion
