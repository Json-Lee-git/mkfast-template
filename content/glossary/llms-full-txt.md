---
title: LLMs-full.txt
description: An expanded version of LLMs.txt that contains the full Markdown content of a website's key pages rather than just links and summaries.
---

**LLMs-full.txt** is the expanded counterpart to the standard [LLMs.txt](/glossary/llms-txt) file. While LLMs.txt provides a curated list of links with one-line descriptions, LLMs-full.txt includes the complete Markdown content of those pages.

## Who should use it

LLMs-full.txt is most valuable for documentation sites, API references, developer tools, and knowledge bases — sites where the actual content is what AI systems need access to.
