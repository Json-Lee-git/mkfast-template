---
title: AI Search Readiness
description: The degree to which a website is technically prepared for AI-powered search systems, crawlers, and answer engines to access, understand, and cite its content.
---

**AI Search Readiness** measures how well a website is prepared for discovery by AI search products like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews.

## What it covers

- AI crawler access (robots.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, etc.)
- AI-readable files (LLMs.txt, LLMs-full.txt)
- Structured data (JSON-LD schema)
- Answer-ready content structure
- Entity clarity and trust signals

## How to check

Use the [AEO Checker](/tools/aeo-checker) or [LLMs.txt Checker](/tools/llms-txt-checker) to audit your site's readiness in under a minute.
