---
title: AI Crawler
description: An automated bot deployed by AI companies to scan and index web pages for training models or generating real-time AI search results.
---

An **AI crawler** is a specialized web crawler (bot) operated by AI platforms to collect web content. Unlike traditional search engine crawlers that index pages for a search results page, AI crawlers may use content for model training, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), or real-time AI search.

## Major AI crawlers

| Crawler | Operator | Purpose |
|---------|----------|---------|
| GPTBot | OpenAI | ChatGPT search, training |
| ClaudeBot | Anthropic | Claude search |
| PerplexityBot | Perplexity | AI search answers |
| Google-Extended | Google | Gemini, AI Overviews |
| Applebot | Apple | Apple Intelligence |
| cohere-ai | Cohere | RAG retrieval |

## Control

AI crawlers respect robots.txt directives. You can allow or block specific crawlers by User-agent name. Some platforms also publish their crawler IP ranges.
