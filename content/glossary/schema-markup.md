**Schema markup** is structured data expressed using the Schema.org vocabulary — a collaborative project by Google, Microsoft, Yahoo, and Yandex. It defines types (Organization, Article, Product) and properties (name, datePublished, author) that give search engines and AI models a precise understanding of what a page contains.

## Why it matters for AI search

Schema markup provides the disambiguation layer that AI models need. When a page uses `Article` schema with `author`, `datePublished`, and `publisher`, the AI knows it is a news article, not a product page or personal blog post — improving the accuracy of citations and summaries.

## Key schema types

- **Identity**: Organization, Person, LocalBusiness
- **Content**: Article, BlogPosting, HowTo
- **Navigation**: BreadcrumbList, SiteNavigationElement
- **Q&A**: FAQPage, QAPage
- **Product**: Product, Review, AggregateRating
