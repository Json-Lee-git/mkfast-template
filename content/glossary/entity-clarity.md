---
title: Entity Clarity
description: How unambiguously a web page communicates what specific person, organization, place, or concept it is about to search engines and AI systems.
---

**Entity clarity** measures how well a page identifies its subject in machine-readable terms. A page about "Mercury" is ambiguous without additional signals. Is it the planet, the element, the car brand, or the singer? Entity clarity means using structured data, context, and explicit identifiers to remove that ambiguity.

## Why it matters for AI search

AI models use entity resolution to decide whether your page answers a given query. If the model cannot confidently identify the entity your page describes, it will not cite your content — even if the text is perfectly written.

## How to improve it

- Use `Organization` or `Person` schema with `sameAs` links to Wikidata, Wikipedia, or social profiles
- Include the entity name prominently in the title and H1
- Use consistent, unambiguous naming across the page
- Link to authoritative external sources that reinforce entity identity
