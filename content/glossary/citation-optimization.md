---
title: Citation Optimization
description: The practice of structuring web content so AI models are more likely to cite it as a source when generating answers, summaries, or search results.
---

**Citation optimization** is the deliberate practice of making your content citable by AI systems. It goes beyond traditional SEO — the goal is not just to rank but to become the source an AI model names and links to in its generated response.

## Key techniques

- **Cite-worthy claims** — Make specific, attributable statements that an AI can quote
- **Primary-source positioning** — Publish original data, definitions, or analysis that others reference
- **Clean attribution** — Show clear authorship and publication metadata
- **Structured data reinforcement** — Use schema markup to back claims with machine-readable evidence
- **External validation** — Earn citations from Wikipedia, academic papers, and industry publications

## Why it matters

When AI Overviews and chatbot search become the primary discovery surface, being cited is the new "page one ranking." Content that is factual, well-structured, and properly attributed is more likely to appear in AI-generated answers.
