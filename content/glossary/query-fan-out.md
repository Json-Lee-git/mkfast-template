---
title: Query Fan-Out
description: A technique that expands a primary search topic into multiple sub-queries, intent clusters, and FAQ questions to capture a wider set of long-tail keyword opportunities.
---

**Query fan-out** is the process of taking a single topic or keyword and generating a structured set of derivative queries that represent real user search intents. A fan-out for "LLMs.txt" might produce sub-queries like "how to create LLMs.txt", "LLMs.txt best practices", and "does my website need LLMs.txt".

## Why it matters for AI search

AI search engines answer specific, long-tail questions better than broad queries. A query fan-out helps you identify the exact questions your content should answer, increasing your chance of being cited as a source in AI-generated responses.

## What a good fan-out includes

- **Fan-out queries** — 5-8 specific long-tail search queries
- **Intent clusters** — Grouped user intents (informational, navigational, transactional)
- **FAQ questions** — The exact questions users ask
- **Content gaps** — Topics your competitors cover that you don't yet
