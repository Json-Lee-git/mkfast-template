**AEO** (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of making web pages easier for answer engines — like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude — to understand, extract, and cite.

## How it differs from SEO

SEO focuses on search engine rankings. AEO focuses on answer engine comprehension and citation. A page can rank well on Google but still not be structured well for AI extraction.

## Key AEO signals

- Technical crawlability (HTTP status, canonical, meta tags)
- AI crawler access (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot)
- Structured data (JSON-LD schema)
- Answer-ready content (H1/H2 structure, FAQ sections, short paragraphs)
- Entity clarity (brand name, Organization schema)
- Trust signals (author, dates, about/contact/privacy links)
