---
title: Structured Data
description: Machine-readable markup added to web pages that helps search engines and AI systems understand the content, entities, and relationships on a page.
---

**Structured data** refers to any standardized format for embedding semantic meaning into web pages. The most common vocabulary is Schema.org, and the recommended encoding is JSON-LD. Structured data turns a page from a bag of words into a knowledge graph of entities, attributes, and relationships.

## Why it matters for AI search

AI models and search engines use structured data to disambiguate entities, understand page purpose, and pull facts into featured snippets and AI-generated answers. Pages without it are harder for machines to interpret confidently.

## Formats

| Format | Encoding | Recommendation |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| JSON-LD | `<script>` tag | Google-recommended |
| Microdata | HTML attributes | Legacy |
| RDFa | HTML attributes | Legacy |
