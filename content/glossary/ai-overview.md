An **AI Overview** (also called AI-generated summary, AI snippet, or generative search result) is a concise answer shown above traditional search results. Google's AI Overviews, Bing Copilot, Perplexity, and ChatGPT search all use this pattern — the user sees an AI-written answer with source citations rather than a list of blue links.

## Why it matters

AI Overviews are redefining click-through patterns. If your content is cited in an overview, users may get their answer without clicking. But if your content is missing from the sources the AI uses, you lose visibility entirely.

## Optimization strategies

- Provide clear, definitive answers early in the page
- Use structured data to disambiguate claims
- Maintain LLMs.txt / LLMs-full.txt to guide AI crawlers
- Use FAQPage schema for question-driven content
