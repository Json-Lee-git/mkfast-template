An AEO checker audits whether a page is structured for answer engines and AI search systems to crawl, understand, extract, and trust. It does not guarantee citations or rankings. It shows whether the page has the technical and content foundation needed for answer engine optimization.

Use an AEO checker before rewriting an important landing page, product page, comparison page, documentation page, or guide for AI search visibility.

## What an AEO checker analyzes

The best AEO checking tools look beyond keywords. They inspect the signals that make a page usable as a source.

| Area | What the checker reviews | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Crawlability | Status code, canonical URL, meta robots, sitemap inclusion | Blocked or non-canonical pages are weak source candidates |
| Structured data | Organization, WebSite, Article, FAQPage, Product, SoftwareApplication | Schema helps machines identify page type and publisher |
| Answer-ready content | Question headings, direct answers, concise paragraphs, FAQ | AI systems need extractable claims and clean sections |
| Entity clarity | Brand, author, product category, sameAs links, About page | The system needs to know who is speaking |
| Trust signals | Dates, reviewer, methodology, references, contact paths | Trust cues reduce ambiguity |
| AI-readable files | llms.txt, llms-full.txt, sitemap, robots.txt | These files expose important public pages in cleaner formats |

## How an AEO checker is different from a normal SEO audit

SEO audits usually focus on search ranking signals, traffic potential, keyword targeting, backlinks, and technical indexability. AEO audits focus on answer extraction and source readiness.

There is overlap. A page that is blocked by robots.txt or missing a canonical URL has both SEO and AEO problems. But answer engines also need short definitions, clear entities, structured summaries, and content that can be quoted or summarized without guessing.

## When to use an AEO checking tool

Use an AEO checking tool when:

- You are publishing a page that should answer a known question.
- You want a product or brand page to be easier for AI systems to understand.
- You are improving pages for Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search readiness, Perplexity, Claude, or other AI-assisted search surfaces.
- You need a prioritized repair list before giving work to a writer or developer.
- You want to validate schema, llms.txt, and answer-ready formatting in one pass.

## What to fix first

Fix access problems before rewriting content. A practical order is:

1. HTTP, canonical, robots, and noindex blockers.
2. Missing sitemap or internal links.
3. Invalid or missing schema.
4. Missing answer blocks and weak question headings.
5. Unclear brand, author, date, or trust signals.
6. Missing llms.txt or broken AI-readable file links.

For a deeper site-file check, use the [llms.txt validator](/tools/llms-txt-checker) after the AEO scan.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an AEO checker?

An AEO checker is a tool that audits whether a page is ready for answer engines and AI search systems. It checks crawlability, schema, answer-ready content, entity clarity, trust signals, AI crawler access, and AI-readable files.

### Is an AEO checker the same as an SEO checker?

No. SEO checkers focus on traditional search optimization. AEO checkers focus on whether a page is structured clearly enough for AI answer systems to understand and extract useful information.

### Can an AEO checker guarantee AI search visibility?

No. AEO checking tools can identify readiness gaps, but they cannot guarantee rankings, traffic, ChatGPT citations, Google AI Overview inclusion, or visibility in any AI search product.
