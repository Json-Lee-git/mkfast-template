## About AI Search Readiness Tools

We build free, lightweight tools to help website owners check their technical readiness for AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude, and Gemini.

### What we offer

- **LLMs.txt Checker** — Validate your LLMs.txt file, check AI crawler access, sitemap, and LLMs-full.txt availability.
- **LLMs.txt Generator** — Create a clean, AI-readable LLMs.txt file from your sitemap or manual inputs.
- **AEO Checker** — Run a technical answer engine optimization audit covering crawlability, structured data, content structure, entity clarity, and trust signals.
- **Query Fan-Out Tool** — Simulate how AI search engines decompose queries into sub-queries, intent clusters, and content gaps.

### Why we built this

AI search is changing how people find information online. Traditional SEO tools weren't built for this shift. We wanted to create accessible, free tools that give website owners a clear, actionable picture of their AI search readiness — without requiring technical expertise.

### Our philosophy

- **Free core tools.** All checkers and generators are free with no sign-up required.
- **Actionable results.** Every audit finding comes with a specific fix you can implement immediately.
- **No bullshit.** We don't sell "AI SEO magic" or promise rankings. We check technical signals that are objectively measurable.
