## Submission Rules

- Use conservative claims: readiness checks, not guaranteed rankings or AI
  citations.
- Prefer pages that can cite the methodology, references, or a specific tool.
- Do not publish public posts or final directory submissions without an
  action-time confirmation.
- Do not send a personal email address unless the owner explicitly approves the
  exact destination.
- Record the live listing URL after each successful submission.
