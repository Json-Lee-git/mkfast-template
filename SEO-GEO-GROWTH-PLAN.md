# SEO/GEO Growth Plan

Product: AI Search Readiness Tools  
Primary conversion: run a free AEO audit, then view the sample report or unlock
the full $19 report.  
Planning date: 2026-06-26

## Operating Rules

- Keep SEO and GEO work separate in planning, but connect both to conversion.
- Prioritize BOFU pages before broad educational pages.
- Every public page should include a direct answer, clear headings, internal
  links, and a next step.
- GEO pages should include FAQ blocks, comparison tables, definitions, and
  citation-friendly paragraphs.
- Do not claim guaranteed rankings, AI citations, traffic, or revenue.
